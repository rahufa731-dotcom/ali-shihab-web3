Institutional Identity & Heritage

About Darunnajath Arabic College (DNAC)

Darunnajath Arabic College (DNAC), Koonanchery, is affiliated with Darul Huda Islamic University (DHIU). For more than four decades, the college has supported students in Islamic and Arabic education, leadership and community life.

Our Vision

"To be an internationally recognized center of excellence in Arabic linguistics, literary criticism, and Islamic jurisprudence, nurturing intellectually capable, morally grounded, and socially responsible global leaders."

Our Mission

  • Impart intensive, high-standard instruction in classical and contemporary Arabic language, literature, and philology.
  • Cultivate advanced research capabilities in Islamic jurisprudence, comparative cultural studies, and professional translation.
  • Integrate cutting-edge digital pedagogy, language laboratories, and modern humanities to enhance global career competence.
  • Foster pluralism, ethical stewardship, community service, and intercultural dialogue among aspiring scholars.

Admission Guidelines

Admissions Are Conducted by Darul Huda Islamic University

Darunnajath Arabic College does not accept applications through this website. Admissions, selection and enrollment are centrally conducted by Darul Huda Islamic University (DHIU).

1

Age Requirement

The applicant must not be more than 12 years old.

2

Educational Requirement

The applicant must have passed 5th standard from a madrasa.

Official Admission Process

Please follow the admission announcements and procedures issued by Darul Huda Islamic University.

Important Notice

For official application dates, documents and selection information, please contact Darul Huda Islamic University directly.
